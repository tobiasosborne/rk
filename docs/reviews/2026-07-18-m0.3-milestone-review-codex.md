## Check-4 ruling

**needs-redesign.** ERROR for an included gitignored-but-present stale payload is a defensible `rk-stricter-intended` policy. But “present in RepoSnapshot” is not a valid global replacement for “Git-tracked”: selective loading also turns tracked stale files outside the include rules into WARN-only passes, and string snapshots cannot guarantee byte-exact hashing. The boundary must expose tracking and raw-byte/hash facts explicitly before the ERROR/WARN policy can be ratified.

The AISM HEAD parity counts and the `node_amended` diagnosis are sound; the post-fix live run removes exactly that linker ERROR, and `3bfca87` is correct. I nevertheless overturn M0.3 acceptance: the zero-rk-bug bar is not met because the general gates retain false-PASS paths, coverage lies, and a crashing input, while the committed acceptance artifact still records one rk-bug and does not justify its historical no-flood ruling.
