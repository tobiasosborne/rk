\section{Approximate homomorphisms of finite-dimensional algebras}

We work throughout with finite-dimensional algebras over the complex numbers,
equipped with a submultiplicative norm.

\begin{definition}
A linear map is a delta-homomorphism when the multiplicative defect is bounded
by delta in the relevant norm.
\end{definition}

The definition above is the one used in every statement of this section; the
constant delta is always taken to be strictly positive.
